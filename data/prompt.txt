{cv_texts}

{job_texts}

{cv_skills}

Job skills:

Rules:
- Keep the answer under 500 words.
- Do not repeat all matched/missing skills.
- Do not invent experience.

- Mention soft skills and motivation only when useful for this specific company/job.
- No cover letter draft.

2. Best application angle

3. Biggest gaps

4. CV changes
Give 3 concrete CV edits for this exact job.

5. Cover letter angle
